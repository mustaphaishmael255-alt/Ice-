    The Difference Yah! Makes

    Comparing traditional food waste practices against our community marketplace approach.

    Without Yah! (The Traditional Problem)

    • Unsold perishable ice-cream melts or goes stale in store coolers.
    • End-of-day batches are routinely discarded into municipal bins.
    • Small artisanal parlours absorb 100% of ingredient & dairy losses.
    • Customers miss out on affordable treats from quality makers.
    • Landfills generate methane from decomposing organic dairy and fruit.

    With Yah! (The Community Solution)

    • Vendors list surplus stock in 30 seconds via the Vendor Dashboard.
    • Nearby residents discover instant discounts up to 50% on Last Scoop.
    • Customers place demo orders for quick evening pickup or delivery.
    • Food businesses recover costs and build lasting customer relationships.
    • Every rescued scoop is dynamically tracked on our community impact board.
