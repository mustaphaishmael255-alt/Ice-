# Yah! — Pitch Deck Outline & Speaker Notes (3-Minute Presentation)

Use these 5 slides if submitting a slide presentation or presenting live to judges:

---

### Slide 1: Title & The Mission
- **Slide Title:** Yah! — Community Ice-Cream Marketplace
- **Subtitle:** Saving Surplus Scoops · Supporting Local Parlours · Fighting Food Waste
- **Presenter:** Roy.tech (WarriorHacks 2.0 Submission)
- **Visual:** Logo, WarriorHacks theme badge, and screenshot of the Marketplace Hero.
- **🗣️ Speaker Note (30s):**
  > *"Good day, judges. Did you know that every single evening, independent ice-cream creameries in our communities discard dozens of unsold artisan scoops simply because they can't sit on the shelf overnight? Small parlours lose money, and families miss out on affordable treats. We created Yah! to solve this with a community-driven surplus rescue marketplace."*

---

### Slide 2: The Core Problem
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **Local Creameries Suffer:** Fresh small-batch dairy has a short shelf-life. Unsold stock = 100% loss.
  - **Consumers Priced Out:** Inflation makes handcrafted treats an unaffordable luxury for many families.
  - **Environmental Harm:** Discarded dairy decomposes in landfills, emitting methane and wasting water.
- **Visual:** Problem vs. Solution comparison graphic.
- **🗣️ Speaker Note (30s):**
  > *"Big delivery apps charge small businesses up to 30% fees and do nothing for food waste. When batches don't sell by 8:00 PM, parlour owners have no quick way to reach neighbors before closing time. That’s pure waste for the owner, for the buyer, and for our local environment."*

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — "Last Scoop"
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **Dual Model:** Normal pricing by day, **Last Scoop** surplus discounts (40%–60% off) by evening.
  - **Countdown Timers & Stock Badges:** Urgency-driven rescue windows before shop closure.
  - **Hyperlocal Directory:** Highlights neighborhood creameries across Accra (East Legon, Osu, Cantonments).
- **Visual:** Screenshot of the `last-scoop.html` deal cards.
- **🗣️ Speaker Note (30s):**
  > *"Yah! introduces the 'Last Scoop' marketplace. In the evening, partner parlours post surplus batches at 40% to 50% discount. Neighbors see the batch, the price, and a live countdown to rescue it before the parlour closes."*

---

### Slide 4: Live Demo (The 60-Second Loop)
- **Live Demo Cues:**
  1. **Show Deal:** Open `last-scoop.html` → Show *Mango Swirl* with live countdown.
  2. **1-Click Rescue:** Click *Rescue Scoop* → Click *⚡ Confirm Demo Order* on `checkout.html`.
  3. **Track Order:** View stage progress on `track.html`.
  4. **Vendor Hub:** Open `admin.html` → Advance order to *Ready for Pickup*.
  5. **Live Impact:** Show `impact.html` incrementing rescued servings in real time.
- **🗣️ Speaker Note (60s):**
  > *"Let's see it in action. A customer spots Mango Swirl for GHS 18 instead of GHS 30. With our 1-click checkout, the order is confirmed immediately. On the parlour's Vendor Hub, the owner sees the order flagged as 'Surplus Rescue' and updates it to ready for pickup. And over on our Community Impact dashboard, our counter updates live: another serving saved, another kilogram diverted from the landfill!"*

---

### Slide 5: Real Community Impact & What's Next
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **Proven Baseline:** 1,428+ servings rescued, 714 kg waste prevented, GHS 24,560 saved by residents.
  - **Self-Sustaining Model:** Sustainable 8% micro-commission vs. 30% predatory aggregator fees.
  - **Future Roadmap:** Expanding to local artisan bakeries, juice bars, and automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts.
- **Visual:** Impact metrics cards + Vendor Honor Roll.
- **🗣️ Speaker Note (30s):**
  > *"Yah! turns what would have been waste into affordable joy for neighbors and recovered revenue for small business owners. It’s local, it’s working right now, and it’s ready to scale to bakeries and fresh produce. Thank you!"*
